When you request a quote, we receive the contact, company, country, product and requirement information you submit. We use it to answer your request, prepare sourcing information and maintain the related business correspondence.

We use Google Analytics to measure page views, approximate region, device type, catalog searches, tool use and quote or WhatsApp conversion actions. Business correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed for quotations, customer service, compliance and legitimate business records. Contact info@tammuzmedical.com to ask about your submitted information.
