Questions about equipping a new surgery

How many instrument sets does one treatment room need?

It depends entirely on your sterilisation cycle and patient throughput. A common approach is enough sets per room to cover a full session between autoclave runs, so a busy surgery may hold three or four of a frequently used set and only one of a specialist set. Start with one per room here, then raise the lines you know you will turn over quickly.

Why does the tool not list what is inside each set?

AsaDental prints set contents in its catalogue, but we only publish specifications we can verify item by item. Rather than show you a list that might be wrong, we show the set as the manufacturer names it and confirm the exact contents with you on quotation. If you need the full breakdown before ordering, ask and we will send it.

Can I mix individual instruments with sets?

Yes. Sets are usually the cheaper way to cover a treatment area, but most clinics add individual references for the patterns they prefer. Build the set list here, then add individual item codes in your message or browse the full catalogue.

Do you supply clinics outside Turkey and Iraq?

Our supply, documentation and inspection process is built around Turkey and Iraq. Send us your requirement anyway and we will tell you honestly whether we can serve your market.

What happens after I send the list?

We check availability, confirm the exact contents of each set, and come back with a quotation including documentation and delivery terms. Prices are always quoted per order — we do not publish price lists.
