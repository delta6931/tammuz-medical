Reference

Reading endodontic size and taper

What does ISO size 25 mean?

It designates a nominal tip diameter of 0.25 mm. The same hundredths-of-a-millimetre relationship applies to the other standard size numbers.

Why do the colours repeat after size 40?

The six-colour sequence white, yellow, red, blue, green and black repeats for larger standard sizes. Sizes 06, 08 and 10 use pink, grey and purple before that sequence.

What does a .04 taper mean?

For a constant .04 taper, diameter increases by 0.04 mm for each millimetre from the tip. A size 25/.04 file is therefore 0.25 mm at the tip and 0.65 mm at 10 mm from the tip.
